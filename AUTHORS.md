# Authors

## Founder

**Muhammed Sheko**
Known as: **SYR-ROOT**

SYROTP was created as an open-source technology project to help
communities where traditional OTP services are unreliable or
unavailable. The project's home region is Syria; the core server
and gateways are designed to be operated inside Syria with local
SIM-card receivers, so end-user verifications never need to leave
the country.

إلى أرواح شهداء سوريا. To the souls of the martyrs of Syria.
